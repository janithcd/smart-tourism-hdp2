# Android device test checklist

Use a device or emulator with Google Play services. Build the current branch, sign in to a test account in Firebase project `smart-tourism-abbb0`, and record the device/Android version and each result. Accounts from the old project do not automatically work in this project. These checks need an installed app; a CI build alone cannot verify them.

| Step | Action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sign up or sign in, open Explore. | Tours, Vehicles, and Guides tabs are visible, with a sample data notice. |
| 2 | Search `Kandy` in Tours, then switch to Guides with the same query. Clear the query. | Tour and guide results match their own category; clearing restores the guide list. |
| 3 | Open a vehicle and a guide in turn. | Each shows its correct category, illustrative rate, details, and booking setup. Car passenger choices stop at 3; van choices stop at 7. |
| 4 | In a listing, tap Save review without stars or text, then enter 4 stars and a comment. Reopen the same listing, edit it, then remove it. | Validation appears; saved rating/comment return for the same account; edit and removal persist. Other listings have no copied review. |
| 5 | Try booking without fields, with an invalid phone, and with a past date/time. | Required, phone, and future date/time errors prevent the draft. |
| 6 | Select a future date/time, valid phone, people, and pickup, then Book Now. | My Activities shows the selected service type, price, date, and time. |
| 7 | After publishing the updated rules, continue to demo payment while online, select a method, and confirm. | No card fields are requested; `demo_bookings` receives the owner-scoped simulation and a device copy appears in Bookings. A notification appears if permitted. |
| 8 | Sign out, sign in as a second account, check Bookings and the first listing's review, then return to the first account. | The second account cannot see the first account's local booking/review; the first account's records remain. |
| 9 | Remove the booking, refresh the admin demo list, and open map and wishlist. | The new demo submission disappears from Firestore and this device; map and wishlist still open. Older local records only remove their device copy. |
| 10 | With no active Firestore `packages`, open Home and Explore; then add an active category and numeric-price package in `smart-tourism-abbb0` and reopen both screens. | Bundled tours appear first with a sample notice. The Firestore tour replaces them once readable; Home shows the category filter, price, image and route/overview from the document. Vehicles and guides remain samples. |
| 11 | Save a live tour to the wishlist, open it from the wishlist, and also open a previously saved numeric-price wishlist item if available. | Both string and numeric prices display without a crash, and the tour details open. The booking and payment path still indicates a simulation. |
| 12 | On a phone with a rotation-vector sensor, open Map and slowly turn the phone while holding it level. Rotate the screen if supported, then leave Map and return. Also try a device or emulator without the sensor. | The magnetic heading and north arrow change with device direction, return after reopening Map, and stop updating off-screen. Without the sensor, the compass shows an unavailable message without a crash. This is a device heading, not turn-by-turn navigation. |
| 13 | After publishing the [reviewed Firestore rules](admin-tour-setup.md), sign in as an admin. Create a hidden category, publish it, select it from Manage tours, add a hidden tour, then publish it. Reopen Home and Explore. | Manage categories and Manage tours appear; the dropdown shows the category name, and published content appears on Home/Explore. |
| 14 | Confirm a demo booking as a tourist and sign in as admin; open Demo bookings, mark it Reviewed, and refresh. | The newest Firestore submission shows the tourist contact and travel details with Reviewed status. No payment or real reservation is implied. |
| 15 | Sign in as a regular tourist and inspect the drawer; attempt direct writes to categories, packages, admins and review status in Rules Playground or emulator. | Admin menu items are absent, writes are denied, and another user's demo booking cannot be read. |
| 16 | Enable Maps SDK for Android and Places API (New) on the restricted Android key with active billing. Open Nearby Places, tap Find near me, allow precise location, then switch Attractions, Food, Stays and Help. | Current coordinates and accuracy appear; nearby results and map markers change by category, with distance and a Directions action. The Google Maps attribution remains visible. |
| 17 | Open Nearby Places on a second account or another device and choose approximate location; also test a denied permission, device location switched off, no network, and no matches. | Approximate location is labeled and searches a wider radius. Denied/offline/empty cases show a useful message without a crash or saving location to Firestore. Directions opens an installed maps app or browser. |
| 18 | Open Settings. Switch between System default, Light, and Dark; force-close and reopen the app after each choice. Change the language and restart again. | The selected theme and language remain selected, the screens are readable, and System default follows the device theme. Partially translated screens may show English fallback. |
| 19 | From Settings, open Android notification and location controls, return after changing a permission, open Edit profile, then sign out. Open About and its links. Press Back from both drawer screens. | Permission summaries refresh, Profile opens, sign-out confirms, About shows the installed version and current data/booking behavior, links open in a browser, and toolbar titles follow the visible screen. |
| 20 | Open Help from the drawer, read the booking and nearby guidance, tap the tourism hotline and office number, then tap the email and source link. Try a device without Gmail. | Both numbers open a dialer without starting a call. Email opens Gmail compose when installed or another mail app otherwise. The source opens the official SLTDA page; Back returns to the previous app screen. |
| 21 | After [Cloud Storage setup](profile-photo-setup.md), open Profile and select a small JPEG/PNG/WebP image; sign out and back in, and inspect the drawer photo. Try a file over 5 MB or without a supported image type, a device without the native picker, and a second account. | Picker falls back to the document picker where needed; only the owner can read or replace the saved image. The photo appears in Profile and the drawer after reopening. Invalid/oversized files show a message; a second account cannot access the first photo through the SDK. |

Record failures with a screenshot, the action taken, and the visible error. For a viva demonstration, say explicitly that services, rates, payment choices, and booking submissions are illustrative rather than live provider transactions. Older records and private reviews remain local.
